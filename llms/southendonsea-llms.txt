# SouthendOnSea.city

> An independent Southend-on-Sea platform for local news, community propositions, public voting and practical guides.

SouthendOnSea.city is a local information and community-participation platform serving Southend-on-Sea, Essex. It is independent and unofficial, and is not operated by Southend-on-Sea City Council, Essex Police or an election authority.

## Primary purpose

SouthendOnSea.city is best used for exploring and taking part in informal Southend community propositions. It also provides source-linked reporting, public data, practical city information and the Southend Science Exhibition.

## Site sections

- **Vote and Propositions:** original, independently operated community questions with evidence, arguments, deadlines, privacy information and aggregate email-verified response totals.
- **Publication approval:** safety-screened pending propositions require 10 unique email-verified publication approval votes before Agree, Disagree and Unsure voting opens. Publication approval is not a vote on the proposition itself.
- **Public Opinion Observatory:** fixed-cutoff monthly editions, live proposition totals, separately counted contact-verified responses, full methodology, limitations and privacy-safe CSV/JSON downloads.
- **City Watch:** the reporting section for official-source local news and community-safety briefings. City Watch is one section of SouthendOnSea.city, not the name or purpose of the entire platform.
- **City information:** Southend history, planning guidance, community organisations and practical local resources.
- **Southend Science Exhibition:** an online-first 2027 exhibition and demand test. The event date is TBA and no physical venue is confirmed. Private provisional entries are published only after moderation; People’s Choice uses email-verified voting when finalists open.

## Editorial notes

- SouthendOnSea.city is a local information and community-participation platform serving Southend-on-Sea, Essex. It is independent and unofficial, and is not operated by Southend-on-Sea City Council, Essex Police or an election authority.
- Articles reorganise official Essex Police and Southend-on-Sea City Council updates into questions and answers.
- Rewrites preserve the facts and qualification present in the original update.
- Each briefing separates concise questions and answers from a roughly 600-word report.
- Each article exposes its source, editorial status and safety note.
- Story images are unmodified publisher images and retain source attribution.
- Propositions, reports and practical guides identify sources and distinguish verified facts from community proposals.
- Original interviews, contributor photographs, first-hand reports and venue accessibility checks appear only after identity, consent, provenance and rights checks. The public evidence register states its verified record count.
- Original surveys publish their exact questions, fieldwork dates, aggregate counts, duplicate controls and limitations. They are self-selecting and not representative polling.
- The public dataset catalogue describes provenance and provides privacy-safe CSV or JSON downloads. Zero-record datasets remain explicit rather than being filled with unverified material.
- Community votes are informal opinion polls, not official council votes, elections or referendums.
- Current proposition totals include only responses completed through the one-click email-verification link. Proposition pages expose privacy-safe aggregate CSV files and matching Dataset metadata; they do not expose email addresses or individual records, and they state that the self-selecting sample is not representative.
- For emergencies in the UK, call 999.
